Process

How we will work together

From first call to first results in about four weeks. After that, it is steady improvement and a clear report every month.

  1. Step 1 · Day 1

    Intro call

    30 minutes on your goals, current channels and budget. If I am not the right fit, I will tell you and point you to someone who is.

  2. Step 2 · Weeks 1–2

    Audit & plan

    I review your accounts, site and tracking, then send a 90-day plan with clear targets and the budget each channel needs.

  3. Step 3 · Weeks 2–4

    Set up & launch

    I fix tracking first, then build and launch campaigns, landing pages and email flows. You approve everything before it goes live.

  4. Step 4 · Every month

    Optimize & report

    Weekly optimization, a one-page report and a strategy call each month: what changed, why, and what we do next.

FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start

Something else on your mind? Send me a message.

How much ad budget do I need?

For most small businesses, $1,500–$3,000 a month in ad spend is enough to test one channel properly. I will recommend a starting budget on our first call, and I will tell you honestly if your budget is too small to work.

How long until I see results?

Paid ads usually show clear signals within 2–4 weeks. SEO takes longer: expect early movement in 2–3 months and meaningful traffic in 4–6 months.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

Retainers start with a 3-month minimum, because that is how long it takes to test and improve campaigns properly. After that it is month to month with 30 days’ notice. Audits and sprints are one-off.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your ad accounts, analytics and website, plus about an hour of your time in the first two weeks. I send a short onboarding checklist once we agree to work together.

Can you work alongside my team or agency?

Yes. I often run paid media or tracking while an in-house team, designer or developer handles the rest. I am happy to share dashboards and join your team chat.
